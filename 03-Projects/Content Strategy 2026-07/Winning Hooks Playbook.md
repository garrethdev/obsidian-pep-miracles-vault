# Winning Hooks Playbook
*Distilled from Social Growth Engineers (56 rules harvested), Virlo small-channel outliers, and our own winners. Full 56-rule set lives in Supabase `content_knowledge_base` (source=sge_harvest_2026_07).*

## Top structural rules (highest evidence)
1. **Repetition beats novelty.** Lock ONE format, repeat it hundreds of times across accounts. Every 100M+ operation does this (Locket ran one hook 838×). Don't invent new angles — duplicate winners.
2. **First-person confession + one concrete detail + a witness.** 8 independent sources + our own data. The witness (mom/husband/friend/coworker) is the engine.
3. **Product OUT of the video** — hook does the emotional setup; CTA soft in caption or pinned comment.
4. **Grade on conversion, not views.** Documented failures: 4.1M views / near-zero downloads. Views without a funnel burn reach.

## Comment-engineering (for Dating especially)
- **Withhold the name** → viewers ask "what's it called?" → answer in pinned reply.
- **Plant a debatable/slightly-wrong claim** → the correction IS the distribution.
- **Third party's own words are the villain** — read a dating profile / a doctor's dismissal aloud and react; audience argues about *them*, narrator stays likable.
- **"comment ME if your doctor ever dismissed your weight"** — one-word identity prompt.

## Hook openers that win
- **Story-tease / time-marker:** "For 6 months…", "pov you locked in for 3 months", a bare date. (Virlo: 5.5× over-indexed on small channels.)
- **Judgment-first, identity-reveal-second:** "But you don't look like you struggled with your weight" → disprove it. (56M / 3 videos.)
- **Institution-as-villain:** "My doctor swore it was just menopause and I missed years."
- **Concede-then-reframe:** "You're right, I took the shortcut. I want to wear the dress from my honeymoon…" — baits critics, rallies defenders, comment war.
- **Question hooks for explainers:** "What are peptides… and why is everyone suddenly using them?" (3,378× on a 5-follower account.)

## Format specifics
- **2-slide B/A:** flattering selfie on slide 1 with the result HIDDEN → reveal on slide 2. Never show the transformation on slide 1.
- **"(tell no one)" carousel:** repeat the refrain every slide, break it on the last ("shock everyone"). 3.1M at 104× median. (Note: our carousel *posting* is broken — use as a video slideshow.)
- **First frame in <1.5s, Friday ~14:00 UTC slots** (winner_patterns: 2,080 avg vs 122–314 other days).

## AVOID (contradicts our data)
- Abstract motivational-quote hooks — our proven loser.
- AI-simulated talking heads on emotional women's-health confession — viewers detect it, views collapse. Use real-face UGC or photo slideshows for the confession; keep AI characters as silent b-roll.
- Over-polished/staged framing — "is this an ad" kills conversion even at millions of views.

## Virlo small-channel truth
Small channels (<50k followers) are where the outliers live — median 33–74× their follower count vs 1.2× for 200k+ accounts. The algorithm distributes on merit. Our best move: 2-slide before/after + dated unresolved story-tease hook, on young accounts.
