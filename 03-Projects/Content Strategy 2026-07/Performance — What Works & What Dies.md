# Performance — What Works & What Dies

*Built from our own post_performance + tt_post_performance data (May–July 2026). All numbers are our accounts.*

## The one rule that explains everything
Every account gets ~110–140 views by default. Content types don't differ in their **floor** — they differ in how often they **break out** (500+ views). So judge content by breakout rate, not median.

## What WINS (proven on our own data)
- **First-person confession + one concrete detail + a witness in the story.** The witness is the engine — the husband, the brunch friend, the woman who noticed the fork. Viewers watch *other people forced to acknowledge her*, not the transformation itself. (Capri dress 29k, "friend who skipped my seat" 1.6k, trina gym-grind 5.9k.)
- **Dating honesty** — "dating is on easy mode when you're skinny." Only reliable comment-driver; best engagement of any type.
- **Medical-dismissal stories** — "three denials, same paperwork." The *direct* peptide pitch flopped (judge score 6); the same subject as a *story about being dismissed* wins.

## What DIES
- **Carousels** — 34% of everything we posted, only 1.7% break 1k, 0.1–0.6% engagement. Dumped 2026-07-03.
- **Eat Whatever** — 51 posts, not one over 400 views. Zero breakouts.
- **Abstract motivation** ("journey", "consistency", "showing up") — 13 posts, none past 320.
- **Third-person narration** (starts with "She…").

## Honest caveats
- 1k views is a *win* at our scale (medians are 100–150). Best original ever = 29k; the 121k was borrowed filler, not ours.
- Grandma B/A's failure is mostly **dead accounts** (shadowbanned), not the content — maya_strong_23 gets normal reach on identical content.
- TikTok is under-tested, not proven-bad: a third of our TT own-content went to 3 shadowbanned accounts (invisible). The healthy TT accounts already hit 700–1,850 on tiny volume.
- **No funnel tracking exists** — nothing measures clicks/conversions/revenue. Views are faith until that's built.
